Why custom, not off-the-shelf

Dashbee is built around the data you already have — not the other way around.

No painful data migration, and no modules you'll never use. Dashbee reads from the source your team already relies on, then puts it all in one view you can read in seconds.

Real-time overview, one screen

Revenue, order count, margin, and low-stock alerts show up as KPI cards with a trend chart — filter by 7, 30, or 90 days and refresh with one click, so you can make a call today instead of waiting for the end-of-month rollup.

Sales & purchases, properly tracked

Add, search, edit, and delete sales and purchase records straight from the dashboard — no spreadsheet formulas that break the moment someone fat-fingers a cell, so your records stay intact exactly when you need them most.

Secure login, role-based access

Sign in with Google or email behind an allowlist — only the addresses you approve can get in. Owner, admin, and staff roles each get their own access boundary, so staff can't open financial data that isn't theirs to see.

Stock counts that stay connected

Inventory is linked to sales and purchases, so you know exactly what's running low without counting shelves by hand or cross-checking three different sheets.

Synced from the Google Sheet you use today

Dashbee's data source is the spreadsheet your team already updates daily — not a new system that forces you to re-enter months of history from scratch.

Works from your phone, any time

The layout adapts automatically to a small screen, so you can check today's numbers between meetings without opening a laptop.

Frequently asked questions

Before you ask, here are the answers.

I'm not very technical — is this hard to use? +

No. The interface is deliberately simple — number cards, charts, and tables you can read without training. If you're already comfortable with Google Sheets, you're already ready for Dashbee.

Is my Google Sheet data safe once it's connected? +

Yes. The connection uses a dedicated service account that only has access to the sheet you share with it, and every table in the database is protected by row-level access rules, so only authorized users can see the data.

If my business grows, can features be added later? +

Yes. "Simple" describes the starting scope, not a permanent limit — additional modules can be scoped and built separately once you need them.

Why go custom instead of an existing tool? +

Off-the-shelf tools have to serve thousands of different businesses at once, so it's normal to end up paying for features you'll never touch, plus a bill that never stops. Dashbee only includes what your business actually needs, and it's paid for up front.

What if I need help after the dashboard is delivered? +

You get hands-on support during the first stretch of real use, to make sure your team is comfortable with it. Changes or new features beyond that are scoped as separate, additional work.
